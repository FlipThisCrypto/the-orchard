# BME280 wiring

The BME280 reports temperature in °C, relative humidity in %, and air pressure in hPa. The Orchard firmware includes a driver that samples all three values.

## Module variants

Breakout boards vary in regulator, level shifting, and pin labels. This guide describes the common four-pin I2C breakout (`VCC`, `GND`, `SDA`, `SCL`); check the documentation for the exact module you have. The project has not verified every vendor's board.

| Variant | Vendor | Pin labels | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common I2C breakout | Varies | `VCC GND SDA SCL` | Verify supply and logic-level specifications for the exact board. |

## Pin-by-pin wiring

The S3 uses GPIO 9 for SCL in the project's PlatformIO profile; GPIO 22 is not exposed on the supported S3 module. Choose the column matching your board and firmware environment.

| Sensor pin | Classic ESP32 WROOM (`freenove_esp32_wroom`) | ESP32-S3 (`freenove_esp32s3`) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| VCC | 3.3V | 3.3V | Confirm the exact breakout supports 3.3V. Do not apply 5V unless its documentation explicitly permits it. |
| GND | GND | GND | Common ground. |
| SDA | GPIO 21 | GPIO 21 | Shared I2C bus. |
| SCL | GPIO 22 | GPIO 9 | Shared I2C bus. |

ESP32 GPIOs are not 5V tolerant. Confirm I2C pull-ups go to 3.3V; a breakout's ability to accept 5V power does not imply its signal pins are safe at 5V. Avoid powering the module until the pin labels and supply range are confirmed.

## Protocol details

- **Bus:** I2C.
- **Addresses:** `0x76` or `0x77`; the driver probes both during startup.
- **Pull-ups:** Use the breakout's I2C pull-ups if present, and ensure they pull up to 3.3V. Do not add another set without checking the board's schematic.
- **Reported fields:** `temperature_c`, `humidity_pct`, and `pressure_hpa`, plus the detected `i2c_addr`.

## Calibration notes

The driver configures 1× oversampling for temperature, pressure, and humidity, with normal mode, filter off, and a 1-second standby interval. Mount the sensor away from direct sunlight, heat sources, and enclosure heat; those conditions affect the measured environment. The firmware does not apply site-specific calibration.

## Bring-up checklist

1. Wire power and I2C according to the matching board column above.
2. Build and flash the matching firmware environment (`freenove_esp32_wroom` or `freenove_esp32s3`). See [firmware/README.md](../../firmware/README.md) for PlatformIO setup.
3. Open the serial monitor at 115200 baud. On boot, the sensor registry should report `bme280` as active. If it is inactive, check power, SDA/SCL wiring, and the selected board profile.
4. After a successful reading, expect the sensor object to contain `temperature_c`, `humidity_pct`, `pressure_hpa`, and `i2c_addr`. The driver omits failed reads instead of publishing zero-filled measurements.

## Known issues

- A breakout at `0x76` or `0x77` is supported; other addresses are not probed by this driver.
- No hardware variant has been independently qualified by this guide. Confirm the exact module's pinout and voltage limits before connecting it.
