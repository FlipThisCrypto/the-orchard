Claim your Tree

Bind a freshly-planted Tree to your wallet. Connect a Chia wallet holding an Orchard Pass, enter the claim code your Tree shows over serial, and it becomes yours on the network.

1Connect your wallet

Uses WalletConnect — works with Sage and Goby. We only ask to read your address and sign a one-line login message; no spend permission is requested.

2Enter the Tree's claim code

The 8-character code printed on the Tree's serial console ([provision] claim code: …).

3Claim

Open-source environmental DePIN on Chia · GitHub · Genesis Passes You plant. The network grows.
