Claim your Tree

Bind a freshly-planted Tree to your wallet. Connect a Chia wallet holding an Orchard Pass, enter the claim code your Tree shows, and it becomes yours on the network.

1Connect your wallet

Uses WalletConnect — works with Sage and Goby. We only ask to read your address and sign a one-line login message; no spend permission is requested. Your connection is shared across The Orchard.

Your Trees

2Enter the Tree's claim code

Find this code on the Tree itself — its screen, or the serial console right after flashing ([provision] claim code: XXXX-XXXX). The code is how you pick which Tree you're claiming — only someone holding that device sees it.
